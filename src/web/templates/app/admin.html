Autonomous Financial Participant Framework

GAAP / IFRS Double-Entry Compliance

Unlike conventional personal expense trackers, SME Finance Copilot operates as an autonomous financial participant. It maintains real-time general ledger balancing, inventory valuation under weighted-average and FIFO methodologies, customer receivables aging (0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days), and automated depreciation schedules.

Gated Document OCR Pipeline

Multilingual Extraction with Review Barrier

Supports parsing of supplier invoices and receipts in English, Chinese (Fapiao - 增值税专用发票), and Bengali (Mushak 6.3 - মূসক ৬.৩). Extraction output is held in a gated staging queue requiring human approval before ledger entry generation, completely eliminating corrupted postings.

SME Loan & DSCR Bankability Evaluator

Covenant Matching & EMI Analysis

Evaluates Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR), comparing flat-rate versus reducing-balance amortization schedules across 6 to 60-month tenures. Automatically evaluates eligibility against statutory SME lending covenants for institutional banking partners (such as BRAC Bank and City Bank).
