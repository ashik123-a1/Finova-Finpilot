Phase 01 • Anti-Pollution Ingestion

Gated OCR & Multilingual Invoice Extraction

Retail invoices are messy: handwritten chits, thermal receipts, Chinese VAT Fapiao (发票), and Bangladeshi VAT Mushak 6.3. SME Finance Copilot extracts structured line items, VAT rates, and supplier TAX IDs.

Mandatory Human Review Gate

OCR extractions are never silently posted to general ledgers. Authorized staff must review and confirm extracted line totals, protecting financial statements against OCR misreads.

Fapiao, Mushak 6.3 & Commercial Invoices Supported
Rolling Origin ARIMA(1,1,1) + LightGBM Regressors
Phase 02 • Predictive Intelligence

Chronological Cash Flow & Runway Forecasting

Avoid running out of working capital when supplier bills arrive. Our ensemble models benchmark past chronological splits, forecasting 7, 14, 30, and 90-day cash intervals with 95% confidence bands.

MASE: 0.42 58% accuracy gain over naive random-walk
WAPE: 0.12 Under 12% error on 30-day liquidity horizon
Phase 03 • Institutional Capital

SME Bank Loan & DSCR Pre-Qualification

Banks reject SME loans because business records lack auditability. SME Finance Copilot deterministically evaluates Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR), stress tests interest rate spikes, and compares products across major commercial banks.

  • Reducing balance vs flat EMI repayment schedules with minor-unit zero rounding loss
  • Statutory SME loan covenants matched for Bangladesh (BRAC, City Bank) and China (ICBC, CCB)
  • 1-click institutional bank PDF loan packet with tamper-evident cryptographic checksums
Real-Time DSCR > 1.25x Bank Suitability Verification
RFM Customer Segmentation & Stock-Aging PO Automation
Phase 04 • Operational Cash Acceleration

Customer Receivables Recovery & Stock Reorders

Tied-up capital in unpaid customer accounts or aging dead-stock kills retail margins. SME Finance Copilot segments customers automatically and alerts you to restock fast-moving SKUs before stockouts occur.

WhatsApp / SMS Reminders 1-click personalized debt chase drafts without fabrication
Auto-Draft Purchase Orders Generates supplier POs the instant stock breaches minimum buffer
Phase 05 • Balance Sheet Audit

Immutable Ledger & Statutory Audit Verification

Every ledger entry is anchored by cryptographic checksums and immutable audit logging. Whether preparing tax filings under Chinese VAT or Bangladesh NBR regulations, the balance sheet remains mathematically verified with zero float errors.

Zero-Loss SQLite WAL Mode

ACID compliance with write-ahead logging guarantees no transaction loss during power cuts or network drops across all retail store branches.

Automated GAAP / IFRS Balance Reconciliation
