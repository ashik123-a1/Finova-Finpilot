### [Slide 1: Title & Vision] (0:00 - 0:45)
*"Distinguished judges and fellow innovators. Across emerging markets, over 400 million micro and small enterprises form the backbone of the economy. Yet, more than 80% struggle with cash flow collapse, predatory interest rates, and an inability to access bank credit.*

*Why? Because they do not have a CFO, they do not have an automated accounting team, and traditional accounting software is purely passive—it records the past, but never participates in the business.*

*Today, we introduce **SME Finance Copilot**: an autonomous, local-first financial operating system that transforms AI from a passive chatbot into an active financial participant."*

---

### [Slide 2: Core Architecture & Zero-Float Integrity] (0:45 - 1:30)
*"To build an AI you can trust with your business, you need three foundational pillars:*

*First: **Zero-Float Accounting**. In our database, every cent, poisha, or fen is modeled as an integer minor unit. Floating-point errors are strictly eliminated. Revenue minus expenses reconciles to the exact penny.*

*Second: **Local-First Edge Resilience**. Our merchants operate in areas with intermittent connectivity. SME Finance Copilot runs 100% locally with zero cloud lock-in. RAG embeddings, OCR extractions, and turnaround models run on local hardware.*

*Third: **Consequential Action Safeguards**. Autonomous AI should advise, but must never commit capital without merchant consent. Every transaction proposed by voice or AI is gated by a cryptographically signed authorization barrier."*

---

### [Slide 3: Solving the Real SME Problem: Loss Turnaround] (1:30 - 2:45)
*"Let us examine a live failure mode that breaks standard LLMs. A merchant enters their shop and says: 'I am in loss, how can I recover?'.*

*Generic AI chatbots hallucinate generic business advice or cite irrelevant bank loan contracts. But SME Finance Copilot executes an 11-domain intent classifier grounded in the merchant's live ledger:*

*It detects that Bismillah Store has generated 1.52 million USD in revenue against 4.86 million USD in operating expenses. But more importantly, it uncovers the root cause: **73.8% of all spending is locked in fixed overheads—rent and salaries**.*

*It immediately formulates a 3-phase turnaround plan:*
1. *Immediate cash defense by mobilizing 89,382 USD in overdue receivables via 1-click WhatsApp reminders.*
2. *Restructuring fixed overheads, showing that a 5% cut directly injects 243,408 USD into cash flow.*
3. *Margin-guarded merchandising, showing that a 10% sales lift adds 152,540 USD.*

*It models the future mathematically, grounded in real ledger actuals."*

### [Slide 4: Bridging the SME Credit Gap] (2:45 - 3:45)

*It calculates real-time Debt Service Coverage Ratios against banking benchmarks, matches loan products across 5 commercial banks, and generates an official Credit Underwriting Packet PDF with watermarked cryptographic hashes ready for bank submission.*

*No more loan rejections due to disorganized records."*
